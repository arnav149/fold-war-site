Fold War

A two-player paper ink war for iPhone.

Privacy

Fold War runs entirely on your device. It collects no data, makes no network connections, and has no accounts, ads, analytics or purchases.

Player names and match progress stay in the app's memory while it's open and are gone when it closes. Nothing is stored or shared.

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released.
